Truck Accidents Attorney Serving Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania & Atlanta, Georgia

   Truck collisions are much different than passenger vehicle crashes, namely because trucks are a lot heavier and the damage they cause is often a lot more significant. Truck collisions are
   also different from everyday car wrecks because there are different safety rules that apply to the safe handling of trucks. Truck drivers, as well as the motor carriers they work for, are
   required to closely adhere to the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Regulations. It is when drivers and carriers choose to operate outside the rules set forth by the FMCSR do we see deadly
   collisions occur on the roads.

Who Can Be Liable for a Truck Accident?

   When filing a personal injury claim after a trucking accident, one of the major obstacles will be proving liability, or fault. In these types of accidents, there are typically more parties
   involved than just you and the truck driver. The trucking company the driver works for, the truck owner, or the cargo loader or manufacturer may also be responsible if they failed to meet
   safety requirements or properly inspect their equipment.﻿
