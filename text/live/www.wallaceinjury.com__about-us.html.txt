Here at The The Law Office of Shaheen Wallace, LLC d/b/a Talon Law Group

﻿

our Philosophy is Simple:

   1. Our Clients Become Family

   ﻿

   If you are calling this office, it is because you or your loved one is severely injured and don’t know where to go or what to do. This time period is often confusing and stressful, especially
   with the prospect of incurring medical bills and taking time off work. So, in order to help you along in this time, we have to be able to understand and empathize. Even more, we treat you the
   way we’d hope someone would treat our loved ones.

   2. We Make Communication Easy

   Communication is paramount to the success of our representation. Everyone that we represent has access to this office via email, telephone, or text. Without communication between attorney and
   client, we would not be able to fully understand and communicate your loss to a jury.

   3. We Are Always Ready to Fight

   Getting full and fair compensation for an injury won’t come easy. The powerful insurance companies will do whatever it takes to pay as little as they can or even not at all. They will often
   try to settle with you before an attorney is even involved. They know that only a qualified injury attorney will understand and demand the real value of the case. It is our mission to take on
   these corporations to fiercely protect your rights and win the fight for your full and fair compensation.
